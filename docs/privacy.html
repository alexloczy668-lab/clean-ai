Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026 · applies to the Clean AI Windows app (website and Microsoft Store versions) and this website.

Clean AI is made by Alex (brand NS_website16), based in Serbia. In this policy "we" means the developer of Clean AI. Contact: loczyalex2.0@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your files never leave your PC. Scanning, duplicate detection, organizing and clean-up all run locally. File contents, file names and folder paths are never uploaded.
  • The only thing the app sends online is your licence key and an anonymous device ID, to check that the licence is valid.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no telemetry.

What we collect and why

DataWhyWhere it is stored
Licence key To check that your trial or subscription is valid. On your PC. The licence server forwards it to Lemon Squeezy to check it and stores only a one-way SHA-256 hash of it (Supabase).
Anonymous device ID — a one-way SHA-256 hash of your Windows machine ID To bind a licence to one PC and to allow one free trial per PC. The hash cannot be turned back into your machine ID and does not identify you. Licence server (Supabase).
Activation dates (when a key or trial was first used on a device, and when it was last checked) To issue and renew licence tokens. Licence server (Supabase).
Email address, name, billing details Only if you buy a subscription. Collected by Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider, to process payment and email you your key. We never see your card details. Lemon Squeezy.
IP address Seen by our hosting providers (Supabase, GitHub Pages) whenever the app or the website connects, as with any internet request. We do not use it to identify you. Provider logs, kept for a short time by those providers.

The Clean AI app itself does not ask for your name or email and has no user accounts. We do not sell, rent or share your data for advertising.

What stays on your PC

Everything the app learns about your files stays on your PC: the scan results, file names and paths, sizes, duplicate groups, the activity log (used for undo) and the quarantine folder that holds removed files until you empty it. These are stored in the app's own data folder (the Windows app data folder) and are deleted when you uninstall Clean AI.

If you installed the website version, the app also downloads a small public file (version.json) from this website to check for updates. That request carries no key, ID or file information. The Microsoft Store version does not do this; the Store handles updates.

Service providers

  • Supabase (licence server and database). Supabase is used to check licences; it is not used to store any of your files or file names.
  • Lemon Squeezy (payments, merchant of record) — see the Lemon Squeezy privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages (hosts this website and the update file).
  • Microsoft, if you get Clean AI from the Microsoft Store, under Microsoft's own privacy statement.

How long we keep it

Activation records are kept while your licence is active and up to 12 months after it ends, so a renewed licence keeps working. Trial records (only the anonymous device ID and the trial dates) are kept so each PC gets one free trial. You can ask us to delete any of these at any time.

Your rights and deleting your data

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you. To delete your account and data:

  • In the app, open Settings → Privacy & your data → Request deletion, or
  • email loczyalex2.0@gmail.com with your licence key or the email you bought it with.

We delete your licence and activation records from the licence server within 30 days and confirm by email. To remove the key from your PC right away, use Settings → Licence → Delete key. Uninstalling the app deletes all local app data. Purchase records held by Lemon Squeezy may have to be kept for tax law; you can also contact Lemon Squeezy directly.

If you live in the EU/EEA or another region with data-protection law, you can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Licence tokens are digitally signed so they cannot be forged or moved to another PC. The licence database is not publicly readable; only the licence server can access it.

Children

Clean AI is a general utility and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top. Important changes are also mentioned in the app's release notes.
