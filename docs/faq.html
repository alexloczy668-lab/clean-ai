FAQ

Questions

Is it really 100% local?

Yes. Scanning, sorting, hashing and the dashboard all run on your PC. The app only goes online for the licence check (your key plus an anonymous device ID, about every 14 days), and it sends nothing about your files. Updates come through the Microsoft Store.

Can it delete something important?

It never deletes outright. "Remove" moves files into a quarantine folder, and every action in the activity log can be undone. System folders are skipped entirely.

What happens if I cancel?

The app asks for a licence key again when your paid period ends. Your files stay exactly where Clean AI put them, and nothing is locked or hidden.

How do I get my licence key?

After checkout Lemon Squeezy emails it to you straight away. Paste it into the app under Settings → Licence. Lost it? Open My Orders and sign in with your email.

How do I cancel or change my card?

Open My Orders and sign in with the email you bought with. Your renewal date is shown in the app, in the bottom-left corner.

Where do I download it?

From the Microsoft Store. Microsoft checks and signs the app, and the Store installs updates for you.
