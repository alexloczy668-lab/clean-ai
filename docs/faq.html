FAQ

Questions

Is it really 100% local?

Yes. Scanning, sorting, hashing and the dashboard all run on your PC. The app only goes online for the licence check (your key plus an anonymous device ID, when it starts and every few hours), and it sends nothing about your files. It keeps working offline for up to 7 days after the last successful check, then needs an internet connection to continue. Updates come through the Microsoft Store.

Can it delete something important?

It never deletes outright. "Remove" moves files into a quarantine folder, and every action in the activity log can be undone. System folders are skipped entirely.

Is there a free trial? When am I billed?

There is no free trial. You pay the full price at checkout and the subscription starts straight away. It renews automatically on the same date every month (monthly plan) or every year (yearly plan) until you cancel.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Any payment, the first one or a renewal, is fully refunded if you ask within 14 days of it. Email loczyalex2.0@gmail.com with your order number. See the refund policy.

What happens if I cancel?

You are not charged again. The app keeps working until the end of the period you already paid for, then asks for a licence key again. Your files stay exactly where Clean AI put them, and nothing is locked or hidden.

How do I get my licence key?

After checkout Dodo Payments emails it to you straight away. Paste it into the app under Settings → Licence. Lost it? Open My Orders and sign in with your email.

How do I cancel or change my card?

Open My Orders and sign in with the email you bought with, or email loczyalex2.0@gmail.com and we cancel it for you. Your renewal date is shown in the app, in the bottom-left corner. Full details are in the terms.

Where do I download it?

From the Microsoft Store. Microsoft checks and signs the app, and the Store installs updates for you.
