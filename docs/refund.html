Refund policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026 · applies to monthly and yearly Clean AI subscriptions.

14-day money-back guarantee. If you are not happy, you get a full refund of any payment, the first one or a renewal, when you ask within 14 days of that payment. No questions asked.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Email loczyalex2.0@gmail.com from the email you bought with.
  2. Include your order number (it's on your receipt) or your licence key.

We refund the full amount, including any VAT, to the payment method you used. The refund is sent through our reseller (the Merchant of Record shown on your receipt). It usually reaches your account within 5 to 10 working days, depending on your bank.

What happens after a refund

  • The refunded subscription is cancelled, so you won't be charged again.
  • The licence key is deactivated, and the app asks for a key again.
  • Your files stay exactly where they are. Nothing is locked, hidden or deleted.

After 14 days

After 14 days a payment is no longer refundable, but you can cancel at any time so you're not charged again. The app keeps working until the end of the period you paid for. How to cancel is in the terms of service.

Your legal rights

This policy adds to your rights under consumer law and doesn't replace them. If you are in the EU, the 14-day guarantee covers at least the same period as your legal right to withdraw.
