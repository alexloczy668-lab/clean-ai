Features

Everything a tidy drive needs

Six tools in one small app. All of it runs on your own hardware.

🗂️

Organizer

Sorts loose files into Photos, Videos, Music, Documents, Installers and Archives. Review mode by default; auto mode when you trust it.

🔍

Duplicates

Checks size, then a quick hash, then a full SHA-256. Only byte-identical files are reported — even when the names are different.

🧹

Old & junk

Temp files, crash dumps, Thumbs.db and anything untouched for 6+ months. Suggestions only — you choose what goes.

📊

Disk dashboard

Drive usage, a treemap of what is eating space, and your largest folders and files — so you know where the gigabytes went.

↩️

Activity log + Undo

Every move, rename and removal is logged with old path, new path and time. Each row has its own Undo button, even after a restart.

📴

Works offline

No sign-in to use it. Pull the network cable and Clean AI keeps working: the licence only needs to go online once every few weeks.
