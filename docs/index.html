Features

Everything a tidy drive needs

Six tools in one small app. All of it runs on your own hardware.

🗂️

Organizer

Sorts loose files into Photos, Videos, Music, Documents, Installers and Archives. Review mode by default; auto mode when you trust it.

🔍

Duplicates

Checks size, then a quick hash, then a full SHA-256. Only byte-identical files are reported — even when the names are different.

🧹

Old & junk

Temp files, crash dumps, Thumbs.db and anything untouched for 6+ months. Suggestions only — you choose what goes.

📊

Disk dashboard

Drive usage, a treemap of what is eating space, and your largest folders and files — so you know where the gigabytes went.

↩️

Activity log + Undo

Every move, rename and removal is logged with old path, new path and time. Each row has its own Undo button, even after a restart.

📴

Works offline

No cloud backend, no sign-in to use it. Pull the network cable and Clean AI works exactly the same.

🔒

Built so it can't wreck your files

Cleanup tools are scary because one wrong click deletes something you needed. Clean AI is designed around that fear.

  • Nothing is deleted outright — removed files go to a quarantine folder you empty yourself
  • Moves never overwrite: a clash becomes report (2).pdf
  • Windows, Program Files and AppData are never scanned
  • Your file names and contents never leave the PC — there is no server to send them to

FAQ

Questions

Is it really 100% local?

Yes. Scanning, sorting, hashing and the dashboard all run on your PC. The app's only network request is checking a licence key when you enter one — that sends the key, nothing about your files.

Can it delete something important?

It never deletes outright. "Remove" moves files into a quarantine folder, and every action in the activity log can be undone. System folders are skipped entirely.

What happens when the trial ends?

The app asks for a licence key. Your files stay exactly where Clean AI put them, and nothing is locked or hidden.

How do I get my licence key?

After checkout Lemon Squeezy emails it to you straight away. Paste it into the app under Settings → Licence. Lost it? Open My Orders and sign in with your email.

What is the Sign in page for?

Sign in with your licence key to see your plan, renewal date and devices, and to manage billing or cancel. You don't need it to use the app.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is that safe?

That SmartScreen message appears for new apps from small developers that haven't built up download reputation yet. Click More info → Run anyway.
